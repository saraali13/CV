This task loads a chest X-ray as a grayscale image and improves the visibility of lung structures. 
Histogram equalization enhances the overall contrast, 
while a JET heatmap converts pixel intensities into colors for easier visual analysis. 
Color balancing corrects the heatmap, and thresholding extracts the densest tissues. 
Logarithmic and power-law transformations are also applied to reveal dark regions and improve soft-tissue visibility. 
All processed results are displayed for comparison and saved in the output folder.
